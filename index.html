LCLA

Language-Conditioned Latent Alignment for vision-language navigation.

Project page Read on arXiv

Align, don’t retrain.VLM image and text features are mapped into the latent space of a trained privileged policy.

Just train the adapter.A lightweight adapter decouples perception from control while the expert policy stays frozen.

Reuse expert behavior.The same expert transfers across sensing modalities and environments, guided by natural language.

Meet the researcher

From mechanical systems to embodied intelligence.

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Mechanical Engineering at Iowa State University and a Graduate Research Assistant. My work spans reinforcement learning for robotic manipulation, robot navigation using LLMs and VLMs, and model predictive control.

Before my Ph.D., I worked as an R&D Engineer at NSDeVil on autonomous navigation for wheeled robots.
